Job Market Paper

A Structural Gravity Analysis of Trade Under Imperfect Competition: Evidence from the Global Tree-Nut Market

Umesh Gautam and Stephen Devadoss

Global tree-nut trade is concentrated among a small set of major suppliers. This paper embeds alternative market-conduct assumptions into a structural gravity framework, compares perfect competition with multiproduct Bertrand and Cournot conduct, evaluates model-implied trade shares against observed allocation, and studies tariff removal under each conduct assumption.

Current result Cournot conduct provides the best fit among the competing specifications, and conduct assumptions materially change predicted trade reallocation and welfare.
Request draft ↗

The analysis uses global bilateral tree-nut trade and policy data, PPML estimation with high-dimensional fixed effects, structural model-fit comparisons, and common tariff counterfactuals across competing market-conduct models. Robustness exercises examine alternative firm-count assumptions.
